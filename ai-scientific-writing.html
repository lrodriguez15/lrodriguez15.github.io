AI for Scientific Writing

Use generative AI and AI agents to plan, research, draft and revise a real short paper — without outsourcing the thinking. You leave on Friday with a paper, a documented AI workflow you can defend, and the habits that make AI helpful instead of dangerous.

generative AI scientific writing IEEE short paper

How this week works

The model assists; you are the author. In this module you may use AI for any step you can explain, check and defend. Everything you submit — every sentence, number and reference — is your responsibility, and every AI step goes in your AI-use log.

In pairs, you write a 2–4 page short paper (IEEE conference template) about a project you have already done — a capstone, a lab, a competition, an internship — using your own data and results. Each session opens with something AI gets wrong, and ends with a short piece of evidence uploaded to Canvas.

Following Tec de Monterrey's ethical guidelines for students on AI: declare all use, never present AI output as your own work, and verify what it tells you. A fabricated or unverifiable reference is a serious fault; undeclared use follows the Tec integrity process.
