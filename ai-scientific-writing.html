AI for Scientific Writing

Inteligencia artificial para textos científicos

Use generative AI and AI agents to write a technical or scientific article — brainstorming, sources, drafting, ethics and references — without outsourcing the thinking.

generative AIscientific writingethics

How this week works

The model assists; you are the author. You may use AI for any step you can explain, check and defend. Everything you submit — every sentence, number and reference — is your responsibility.

All work is individual: each of you builds one technical or scientific article during the week, with daily graded work and individual feedback. Allowed tools: TECgpt, Agent Studio, Gemini, Copilot (institutional) and Zotero or Mendeley for references.

Following Tec de Monterrey's ethical guidelines for students on AI: declare all use, never present AI output as your own work, and remember that AI is not a reliable source — validate what it tells you. Undeclared use follows the Tec integrity process.
