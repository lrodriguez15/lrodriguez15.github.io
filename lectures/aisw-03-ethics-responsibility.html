Why ethics starts with how the model works

An LLM picks the most probable next word; it does not look facts up and does not know whether a reference exists. It sounds just as sure when it is right as when it invents. Fluent is not true — and the duty to check cannot be delegated.

Advantages and limitations

Helps you: order ideas, propose a structure, fix language, translate (checking terms), summarise and compare sources you provide, critique your draft. Limitations: invented data and references, bias, a knowledge cut-off, a flattened voice, privacy risks, and dependence — if you don’t practise, you don’t learn.

Ethical use, grey zone and misuse

Ethical (and declared): language editing, asking for a structure and critiquing it, summarising sources you read, asking for counter-arguments. Grey zone: rewriting whole paragraphs, translating a whole article, explanatory figures, AI in data analysis — decide and justify. Misuse: presenting AI text as yours, inventing data, citing what you never opened, hiding AI use, hidden instructions to fool reviewers.

Real cases

  • Mata v. Avianca (USA, 2023): lawyers cited court cases invented by ChatGPT and were fined US$5,000.
  • Deloitte Australia (2025): a government report with non-existent references and a fabricated court quote; corrected after a researcher spotted it, with a partial refund.
  • AI figure retracted (2024): a Frontiers article with Midjourney figures — declared, but never checked.
  • Hidden prompts (2025): Nikkei Asia found 17 arXiv preprints with white or tiny text asking AI reviewers for a positive review.

What publishers ask for

Across Springer Nature, Elsevier (policy updated August 2026), IEEE and Taylor & Francis: AI is not an author and the authors are responsible. Disclosure goes in Methods, Acknowledgments or a declaration at submission, naming the tool and how it was used; basic language editing is usually exempt or only recommended; AI-generated research images are not allowed (explanatory diagrams may be, with a caption note, depending on the publisher). Always read the specific journal’s policy.

Declaring and citing AI

AI use statement: While preparing this work I used [tool, version] to [purpose and sections]. I reviewed and edited the content and take full responsibility for the result. APA 7: Anthropic. (2026). Claude [Large language model]. https://claude.ai. AI is not a source — cite the original paper you opened.
